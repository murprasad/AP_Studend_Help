# PrepLion Demand Priority Cycle — 2026-06-23

## Mandatory demand-research protocol

Do not recommend building, retiring, or prioritizing an exam from one community or from candidate
population alone. Every recommendation must triangulate current evidence from:

1. Reddit, including the exam-specific community and larger adjacent communities.
2. At least two non-Reddit student communities, such as College Confidential, AllNurses,
   Student Doctor Network, DegreeForum, school/program forums, Facebook groups where accessible,
   or other active college/career communities.
3. Search-intent evidence: trend direction, query volume where available, and current result/competitor
   density.
4. Commercial evidence: competitor products, pricing, review volume, paid-course activity, and
   advertising intensity.
5. Institutional evidence: schools/programs requiring the exam, administration volume, seasonality,
   and official exam changes.
6. PrepLion evidence: registrations, selected tracks, sessions, retention, checkout attempts,
   conversions, support requests, and waitlist signups.

For each source, record the collection date, sample size, recency, user intent, and limitations.
Separate:

- **Exam population** — people who take the exam.
- **Community activity** — people discussing preparation.
- **Purchase intent** — people actively seeking or buying help.
- **Product fit** — demand PrepLion can credibly serve.

A build recommendation requires evidence from at least three source classes, including one direct
student/forum source and one commercial or behavioral source. If evidence conflicts or is missing,
label the niche **discovery only**, not recommended.

## Decision

Separate calendar urgency from sustained market demand:

- **Immediate (next 30 days): ACT > SAT > CLEP**
- **Sustained market: SAT > ACT > TEAS > CLEP > PSAT > AP**
- **Build priority after current trust blockers: PSAT > selective AP > TEAS relaunch**
- **Retire/hide: ACCUPLACER**

## User pain that should control roadmap

Across SAT, ACT, and CLEP, demand is not primarily for more question volume. The recurring need is:

1. Realistic exam-format practice.
2. Reliable answer keys and explanations.
3. Weak-area diagnosis.
4. A credible readiness or projected-score signal.
5. Pacing and timed-practice support.

Therefore, fidelity and trust fixes rank above bank expansion or marketing spend.

## Operating sequence

1. Finish ACT authenticated browser and figure-aware certification before July 11.
2. Keep SAT regression/fidelity coverage green and begin the August acquisition ramp.
3. Maintain CLEP as the year-round PrepLion core.
4. Build PSAT from the certified SAT bank for an August/September launch.
5. Keep TEAS unavailable until its item-type and Science trust gates pass.
6. Begin selective AP work after fall-exam readiness is secured.
7. Hide ACCUPLACER; do not spend further QA/content capacity without evidence of demand.

### Non-Reddit filter

If Reddit is removed from the evidence set, the ranking should be driven by official test-owner pages, competitor catalogs, school-admission use cases, and institutional mechanics only. Under that filter:

1. `CLEP` stays highest for PrepLion because it is the clearest credit-savings product and maps directly to a trust-first optimizer.
2. `TEAS` stays high because ATI publishes a substantial prep catalog and the exam is a direct nursing-admissions purchase funnel.
3. `GED` becomes stronger than social chatter suggests because it is a four-subject, official high-school-equivalency product with permanent adult-ed demand.
4. `HESI A2` remains viable as a nursing-admissions adjacency, but it is weaker than TEAS because school-specific requirements fragment the market.
5. `AP selective` is a real official market, but it is broad and seasonal, so it should stay selective rather than universal.
6. `PSAT/NMSQT` remains a cheap extension of SAT infrastructure and has a real school-administered window.
7. `NCLEX` is very large but materially higher risk on trust, clinical accuracy, and expert-review cost.
8. `ASVAB` is still discovery only without stronger non-Reddit purchase-intent evidence.

What changes most when Reddit is removed is the confidence level, not the top two or three exam families. The biggest impact is that weaker or speculative candidates lose priority faster, especially where the only prior signal was forum chatter.

### Reddit-inclusive read

When Reddit is restored as one of several evidence classes, it mainly strengthens the high-frequency student-exam markets:

1. `ACT` and `SAT` get the strongest day-to-day support because the subreddits surface constant score-help, pacing, and retake requests.
2. `CLEP` gets additional confirmation of high-intent, self-directed credit-saving demand.
3. `TEAS` still ranks well because the forum signal aligns with the commercial nursing-admissions market, not because of Reddit alone.
4. `PSAT/NMSQT` remains secondary but real, mostly as a low-cost extension of the SAT funnel.
5. `AP selective` stays valid, but the signal is more seasonal and course-specific than SAT/ACT.
6. `HESI A2`, `GED`, and `NCLEX` are better treated as adjacent markets that need cross-checking against official and commercial signals before promotion.
7. `ASVAB` still does not clear a build threshold on Reddit alone; forum size is not enough without conversion evidence.

### ASVAB — discovery only; demand not established

The official ASVAB program reports more than one million administrations annually, but that is not
the same as demonstrated demand for a paid PrepLion product. The direct Reddit community sampled
(`r/ASVAB`) has only about 620 subscribers. Individual posts show real pain around minimum AFQT
scores, retakes, pacing, and job-specific line scores, but the observed community volume is too weak
to justify a build priority.

The differentiated product should not be a generic ASVAB question bank. It should provide:

1. Target branch and desired MOS/rating/job.
2. Required AFQT and line-score targets.
3. Diagnostic by contributing ASVAB subtest.
4. A study plan weighted toward the subtests that unlock that job.
5. Timed CAT-style practice and a clear readiness signal.

PrepLion can reuse substantial arithmetic, algebra, vocabulary, reading, and general-science
infrastructure. Auto/shop, mechanical comprehension, electronics, spatial items, branch-specific
score rules, and diagram fidelity require new official-source research and visual QA.

**Priority decision:** do not build or rank ASVAB as the strongest adjacent niche. Keep it in
discovery only. Advancement requires stronger evidence: sustained search volume, activity across
larger recruiting communities, paid competitor traction, interviews, or a conversion-tested waitlist.
Do not market or pre-sell it.

Secondary discovery candidates:

- **HESI A2:** a plausible nursing-admissions adjacency, but not build-approved. Mometrix reports
  nearly 10,000 users of one free HESI practice test over eight months (~1,250/month on that surface)
  and lists multiple nursing programs with explicit HESI cut scores. This proves some active prep
  usage, not total market size or PrepLion willingness to pay. Reddit demand was not reliably
  measurable in this cycle.
- **NLN NEX:** insufficient evidence. No trustworthy candidate-volume figure, paid-conversion signal,
  or meaningful Reddit/forum activity was established. Do not prioritize or bundle it with HESI
  without separate validation.
- **GED:** large, year-round adult market, but requires essay scoring and several interactive item
  formats, making it a materially larger build.
- **TSIA2 / PERT:** pursue only with verified state/institution partnerships or search-conversion
  evidence; avoid another low-demand Accuplacer-style investment.
